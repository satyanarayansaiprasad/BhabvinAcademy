FAQ

Quick answers.

The most common questions, answered upfront.

Do you offer refunds if I'm not satisfied? +
Yes. We offer a full refund within 7 days of purchase if you haven't completed more than 20% of the course. Just email us and we'll process it promptly — no questions asked.
How long do I have access to a course after purchasing? +
Lifetime access. Once you purchase a course, it's yours forever — including all future updates we make to the content. You can revisit it anytime from any device.
Are the courses suitable for complete beginners? +
Many of our courses are designed for beginners with no prior experience, while others require some foundational knowledge. Each course page clearly states the required skill level so you can choose the right starting point.
Can I access courses on mobile? +
Absolutely. BhavinAcademy is fully responsive and works smoothly on smartphones and tablets. You can learn on the go from any modern browser without needing to install an app.
Do you offer group or corporate pricing? +
Yes! We offer discounted plans for teams of 5 or more, with a centralised admin dashboard, progress tracking, and the option for custom training paths. Use the contact form above and select "Team / Corporate Training" as your topic.
Will I receive a certificate upon completion? +
Yes. Upon completing a course, you receive a verified digital certificate from BhavinAcademy that you can share on LinkedIn or include in your resume. Certificates include your name, course title, and completion date.
